About

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of British Columbia, advised by Prof. Laks V. S. Lakshmanan. I work on model efficiency, agentic systems, and LLM unlearning. My research teaches large models to forget what they shouldn't while staying capable and efficient, and builds agents that reason and plan reliably as they grow more autonomous.

Before UBC, I spent nearly three years as an AI Engineer at Accenture Japan, shipping production machine learning across LLM fine-tuning, NLP, recommendation, and computer vision. Earlier, a research stint at York University focused on Bayesian optimization for materials discovery. That mix is how I like to work: rigorous ideas that actually execute in the real world.

Outside the lab, I'm usually exploring a new city on foot, chasing a good cup of coffee, or trading stories from a path that's run from Kanpur to Tokyo to Vancouver.

Research Interests

My work centers on two questions: how do we make large models radically more efficient, and how do we build agentic systems that reason and plan reliably enough to trust?

Model Efficiency

Compression, pruning, quantization, and unlearning that shrink large models and cut inference cost while keeping the capability that makes them worth running.

Agentic Systems

Agents that decompose problems, plan multi-step actions, and reason reliably toward a goal instead of reacting a token at a time.

Agent Evaluation

Rigorous ways to measure what agents actually do, beyond a single benchmark number.

Adaptive AI

Models that adapt as the world shifts under them, allocating compute where it matters most.

RDumb++: Drift-Aware Continual Test-Time Adaptation

Himanshu Mishra

ICLR 2026 Workshop (CAO & TTU) · 2026

A drift-aware adaptive-reset framework for continual test-time adaptation: it uses entropy and KL-divergence to detect distribution drift, improving accuracy by 3.8% while cutting resets 35% and recovering 20% faster under domain shift.

QUAIL: Quantization-Aware Unlearning for Mitigating Misinformation in LLMs

Himanshu Mishra, K. Mehreen

arXiv preprint (arXiv:2601.15538) · 2026

Ensures LLMs truly forget targeted data even after 4-bit quantization, using a logit-space hinge loss and quantization-bucket analysis. This prevents the catastrophic knowledge recovery that breaks prior unlearning methods while keeping the model useful. Evaluated on MUSE and a Twitter misinformation benchmark.
